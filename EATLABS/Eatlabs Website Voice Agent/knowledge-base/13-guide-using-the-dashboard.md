## The dashboard sections

> Spoken: The menu down the left has Dashboard, Reservations, AI Calls, Guest CRM, Marketing, Loyalty, Events, Reviews, Food Menu, Insights and Settings. Each person sees only what their role allows.

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Dashboard | Today at a glance |
| Reservations | The agenda, floor plan and waitlist |
| AI Calls | Every call, answered or missed |
| Guest CRM | Everyone who has booked, called or visited |
| Marketing | Campaigns, social posts and ads |
| Loyalty | Points, rewards, offers and vouchers |
| Events | Ticketed nights and check-in |
| Reviews | Google reviews and private feedback |
| Food Menu | Dishes and table QR codes |
| Insights | Reports |
| Settings | Outlets, team, phone, WhatsApp, payments, billing and support |

Each person sees only the sections their role allows. A host starts on Reservations and never sees billing.

## Overview and Needs attention

> Spoken: Overview shows today's covers, bookings, floor and calls. Needs attention lists what somebody should act on — check it at the start of each service.

- **Dashboard → Overview** shows today's covers and bookings, how the floor looks right now, today's calls, and how many guests are new or returning. Each panel links to the screen behind it.
- **Dashboard → Needs attention** lists what someone should act on — a guest running late, a callback that is due, a low review. **Check it at the start of each service.**

## Running the day on the agenda

> Spoken: The agenda is the day's list of bookings. From there you add bookings by hand, change or cancel them, and mark guests arrived, seated or no-show.

**Reservations → Agenda** is the day's list of bookings. From it a team can:

- Add a booking by hand — for a walk-in, or a guest who rang the landline.
- Open a booking to change the time, party size or table, add a note, or cancel it.
- Mark guests as **arrived, seated or no-show**.
- Export the day's bookings.

Each booking shows a small icon for where it came from.

## Reading the floor plan

> Spoken: The floor plan draws your tables coloured by what is happening at each — free, seated, arriving soon or running late.

**Reservations → Floor plan** draws the tables as they are laid out, coloured by what is happening at each: **free, seated, arriving soon or running late**. With a POS connected, it also shows when a bill is open or paid. **Edit layout** changes the layout.

## Working the waitlist

> Spoken: When you are full, guests scan a QR code at the door to join the waitlist. They watch their place in the queue on their phone and get a WhatsApp message when the table is ready.

When the restaurant is full, guests scan a QR code at the door to join the waitlist. They see their place in the queue update live on their phone, and get a "your table is ready" message on WhatsApp. The team manages the queue in **Reservations → Waitlist**.

## What the AI Calls tabs show

> Spoken: The call log holds every call with a transcript, and the other tabs cover callbacks due, missed-call recovery, intents and quality, and the hunt group.

| Tab | What you find there |
|---|---|
| Call log | Every call, live: who called, what they wanted, what happened, and the booking it made. Each has a written transcript. |
| Callbacks due | Guests waiting to be called back. One tap calls them. |
| Missed-call recovery | Missed callers, and whether the WhatsApp link brought them back. |
| AI intents & quality | What guests ask for most, and how well the AI handled it. |
| Hunt group & routing | Who rings, in what order, and the button to make a test call. |

Card numbers and other sensitive numbers are removed from transcripts before they are saved.

## How a call is answered

> Spoken: Staff phones ring first, up to 6 per outlet. If nobody answers or you are closed, the AI host picks up and books the table.

1. A guest calls the EatLabs number for the outlet.
2. Staff phones ring — one by one or all together, **up to six phones per outlet**, with different people for different shifts.
3. If nobody answers, or the restaurant is closed, the **AI phone host** answers in English, Hindi or Kannada, checks live availability and books the table.
4. If the guest needs a person, the AI passes the call to staff **with a short summary first**. If still nobody answers, a callback is added to the list.

A guest whose call was missed automatically gets a WhatsApp message with a booking link, their number already filled in.

## Inviting the team and setting roles

> Spoken: Invite people from Settings, Team and roles. They get an email, set a password and sign in, and the invitation lasts 7 days.

- **Settings → Team & roles → Invite member.** Enter their email, and their mobile to send the invitation on WhatsApp too.
- Choose their **role**, and which outlets they work at.
- They open the invitation, set a password and sign in. The invitation lasts **7 days**.

Ready-made roles cover most restaurants: **Owner, Outlet manager, Host, Marketing and Read-only.** What each role can do is adjustable in **Settings → Team & roles → Roles**.

Removing someone from the team ends their access straight away. **Settings → Audit log** shows who changed what, and when.

## What is shared across outlets, and what is not

> Spoken: Guest profiles, loyalty points, segments and campaigns are shared across all your outlets. The phone number, floor plan, menu and opening hours are set for each one.

| Shared across all outlets | Set for each outlet |
|---|---|
| Guest profiles | Phone number and AI host |
| Loyalty points | Floor plan and tables |
| Segments and campaigns | Menu |
| Your team (with access per outlet) | Opening hours and shifts |

An event can run at several outlets at once, and a sister outlet can be promoted to another's guests.

## What guests see on their own phone

> Spoken: Everything guests do happens on their own phone from a link or a QR code. There is no app to download and no account to create.

- **Booking.** By calling, on WhatsApp or Instagram, through Google, or on the restaurant's booking page. They choose date, time and party size, enter name and mobile, pay a deposit if asked by UPI or card, and get a WhatsApp confirmation linking to their booking — from which they can check or cancel it any time before arriving. Deposit refunds follow the restaurant's policy automatically.
- **Waitlist.** Scan the QR code at the door, enter name and party size, and watch their place in the queue update live.
- **At the table.** Scan the table QR code to open the menu in English, Hindi or Kannada, filter for their diet, ask the AI food concierge, and order from their phone. The whole table shares one bill.
- **Events.** Choose tickets and extras from the event link, pay by UPI or card, and get the ticket on WhatsApp.
- **Loyalty.** A loyalty card opens from a QR code showing points and rewards. Guests can buy vouchers online, and type "points" on WhatsApp for their balance.
- **After the visit.** A short feedback request on WhatsApp. Happy guests are invited to leave a Google review; unhappy ones reach the restaurant privately first.

Guest data stays in India. Guests only get marketing if they agreed to it, can reply **STOP** at any time, and can ask for their data to be deleted.
