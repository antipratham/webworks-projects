## Rights a person has under the DPDP Act

> Spoken: You can ask for a copy of your data, have it corrected, have it erased, withdraw consent for marketing, nominate someone to act for you, or complain to our grievance officer.

Under the DPDP Act a person may:

- **Access** — get a copy of their personal data and a summary of how it is processed.
- **Correct** — have inaccurate or incomplete data fixed and outdated data updated.
- **Erase** — have personal data deleted where it is no longer needed and no law requires it be kept.
- **Withdraw consent** — for anything consent-based, such as marketing. One tap in any marketing message, or ask. Withdrawal reaches every channel immediately.
- **Nominate** — name someone to exercise their rights if they die or become incapacitated.
- **Complain** — to EatLabs' Grievance Officer, and then to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## What to include in the request

> Spoken: Four things — the phone number you used with the restaurant, the restaurant's name, what you want deleted, and a way to reply to you. You don't have to give a reason.

1. **The phone number used with the restaurant.** This is the key that finds the record across bookings, calls, messages and loyalty.
2. **The restaurant's name** (and the outlet, if known). Each restaurant's data is separate — a request can cover one restaurant or every restaurant on EatLabs that holds you. Say which.
3. **What should be deleted** — everything, or just part: marketing consent, a call recording, a dietary note, a review reply.
4. **An email address or number to reply on.**

No reason is required, and EatLabs will not ask for one.

## Identity verification

> Spoken: We send a one-time code to the number on the record. If that number no longer works, we'll find another way — a payment reference, or the date and time of a booking.

A **one-time code** is sent to the phone number on the record, read back or replied with. If that number no longer works, alternatives are accepted — a deposit payment reference, the date and time of a specific booking, or the email the confirmation went to.

EatLabs **will not delete on an unverified request**. The stated reasoning: a phone number is not a secret, and without a check anyone who knew it could erase another person's bookings, loyalty points or table tonight.

## How long deletion takes

> Spoken: We acknowledge within 72 hours, usually verify the same day, and deletion runs across every store within 7 days of verification.

| Step | When |
|---|---|
| Acknowledgement | Within 72 hours |
| Verification | Usually the same day |
| Deletion runs across every store | Within 7 days of verification |
| Written confirmation of what was deleted | Immediately after |
| A request needing the restaurant's decision | Up to 30 days, with an explanation |

Deletion is one orchestrated job that walks every place the data could be — guest record, bookings, call recordings and transcripts, message history, provider webhooks, loyalty, tickets, campaign audiences, automation runs and file storage. It is not a single row removed with the rest left behind.

## What gets deleted and what must stay

> Spoken: Your profile, call recordings, transcripts and loyalty balance are deleted outright. Bookings stay but with your name and number stripped, so the restaurant's record of how busy a night was doesn't change. Tax records stay eight years with personal fields stripped, as Indian law requires.

| Data | What happens |
|---|---|
| Guest profile — name, phone, email, dietary notes, allergies, occasions | **Deleted outright** |
| Call recordings | Audio and every version deleted |
| Call transcripts | Deleted |
| Number on calls and messages | Removed; delivery counts stay as numbers with nobody attached |
| Bookings and visits | Name and number stripped; the row stays as an anonymous cover |
| Loyalty balance and redemptions | Deleted — an unredeemed balance goes with it |
| Campaign and automation records | Entries deleted; the campaign's reported reach left as it was |
| Public Google / Zomato reviews | EatLabs tombstones its copy. The public review belongs to that platform and must be removed there |
| Payment, refund and ticket records | Kept, personal fields stripped to the legal minimum. Indian tax law requires 8 years |
| Audit record of the deletion itself | Kept — holds no personal data, exists to prove the erasure happened |

Aggregate figures — covers served, revenue by night, dish popularity — never identified anyone and are not reversible. They stay.

## If you only want the marketing to stop

> Spoken: If the problem is just marketing, you don't need deletion. Tap opt-out on any marketing WhatsApp message or reply STOP to a marketing SMS. It takes effect immediately across every channel, and your bookings and points stay as they are.

Deletion is a big step and often not what is actually wanted. If the problem is marketing:

- Tap the **opt-out button** on any marketing WhatsApp message, or reply **STOP** to a marketing SMS.
- It takes effect **immediately and across every channel at once** — WhatsApp, SMS and email.
- Bookings, loyalty points and visit history stay exactly as they are.
- Messages about a booking actually made still arrive. Those are not marketing.

Consent can also be withdrawn by writing to privacy@eatlabs.io.

## Requests routed to the restaurant

> Spoken: If your data is held for a restaurant, we route the request to them, act on their instruction, and tell you we've done it.

Where data is held for a restaurant, EatLabs routes the request to that restaurant, acts on its instruction, and tells the person it has done so.

Response commitments: acknowledge within **72 hours**; complete within **7 days** for an erasure, or **30 days** for anything needing a restaurant's involvement. A grievance gets a response within **30 days**; if unsatisfied, the person may complain to the **Data Protection Board of India**.

## Children

> Spoken: The dashboard is for restaurant staff and isn't intended for anyone under 18.

The dashboard is for restaurant staff and is not intended for anyone under 18. EatLabs does not knowingly create accounts for children.

A restaurant must not use the platform to collect a child's personal data for tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted advertising, and must have verifiable parental consent where the DPDP Act requires it.

## Cookies on the website

> Spoken: The marketing site uses only a session cookie for forms and Google Analytics. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

The marketing site uses only a session cookie for a form being filled in, plus **Google Analytics 4** for aggregate traffic measurement. No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. Browser controls will block or clear them and the site still works.

The dashboard and guest app use a strictly necessary cookie to keep a user signed in; there is no way to use them without it.
