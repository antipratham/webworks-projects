## One availability engine — no double-booking

> Spoken: Every channel checks the same availability engine, and it's enforced in the database. Two channels physically cannot take the same table.

All booking sources check **one availability engine, enforced at the database level**, so double-bookings are impossible. This holds across outlets in a group too — every outlet checks the same engine.

This is the answer to "can double-bookings happen?" — No. It is enforced in the database, not by convention or by polling between systems.

## Booking sources that feed the agenda

> Spoken: Phone, the AI host, WhatsApp, your website, Instagram, Google, walk-ins, events, and Zomato and Swiggy — all in one agenda.

Sources: phone, AI host, WhatsApp, website, Instagram, Google, walk-ins, events, and Zomato/Swiggy.

The agenda groups the day into services — for example Lunch, Hi-Tea and Dinner — and each booking row shows the channel it came from, the guest, party size, table, visit count and any note (window seat, anniversary, deposit paid, VIP).

## The live floor plan

> Spoken: There's a visual floor plan with live table status pulled from your POS — you drag to seat, see turn times, and block zones for events.

- Visual floor plan with **live table status from the POS** (open, seated, paid, free).
- Drag to seat.
- **Turn-time estimates per party size.**
- **Zone and event blocks** — block part of the room for an event.
- The AI host books only what is genuinely free.

Views available on the reservations screen: Upcoming, Seated, Cancelled, Finished, plus search by name, phone or table.

## Waitlist and queue management

> Spoken: Guests join the waitlist on WhatsApp, the web, or a QR code at the door. They see their position in the queue and get a message the moment a table is ready.

Guests join the waitlist via **WhatsApp, web, or a QR code at the entrance**. They can track their live queue position and get an instant notification when a table becomes available ("table ready" on WhatsApp).

The waitlist sits beside the agenda on the same screen, showing the number of parties waiting and who is next.

## Automatic guest messages

> Spoken: Confirmations, updates and cancellations go out automatically, plus reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before with Confirm or Cancel buttons. If WhatsApp can't deliver, it falls back to SMS.

Automatic messages sent around a booking:

- confirmation
- update
- cancellation
- **reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before**, each with **Confirm / Cancel** buttons

Messages **fall back to SMS** when needed.

## Deposits and no-shows

> Spoken: You can require a deposit by party size, day, shift or date. Guests pay by UPI or card on Razorpay, and refunds follow your policy automatically.

Deposit rules can be set by **party size, weekday, shift or date**. Guests pay by UPI or card on Razorpay. Refunds follow the restaurant's own policy automatically.

A published customer result: deposits for parties of 8+ cut weekend no-shows "from about 18% to under 5%".

Note: deposits, cancellation windows, no-show rules and refund policies are the restaurant's to set and apply — EatLabs applies them, it does not define them.

## Multi-outlet reservations

> Spoken: Groups get one booking page across every outlet — guests pick a location and book in three taps.

For restaurant groups:

- One group booking page listing every outlet, cuisine and time slot; guests pick a location and book in three taps.
- Duplicate guest records across channels are detected and merged.
- Per outlet you set: phone numbers, floor plans, menus and hours.
- Group-level events can run across outlets, sharing the venue, with ticket revenue settled per outlet automatically.
