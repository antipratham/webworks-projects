## What EatLabs is

> Spoken: EatLabs is an AI restaurant operating system for Indian dine-in restaurants. It puts calls, WhatsApp, bookings, the menu, loyalty and reviews in one place, so you are not juggling five apps.

EatLabs is an AI restaurant operating system built for Indian dine-in restaurants. It brings a restaurant's customer communication and growth tools into one platform: phone calls, WhatsApp, bookings, table and floor management, the QR menu, loyalty, marketing and reviews.

The positioning line used on the site is: "Your restaurant has too many apps. We fixed that."

It is built in Bengaluru by WebWorks Co.

## Who operates EatLabs

> Spoken: EatLabs is operated by Chevalbleu Labs, a private limited company based in Bengaluru.

The legal entity behind EatLabs is **CHEVALBLEU LABS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED**, registered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. The site footer carries "© 2026 CHEVALBLEU LABS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED · EatLabs · Bengaluru, India".

The product is built by WebWorks Co, in Bengaluru.

## What happens after someone enquires

> Spoken: We come back to you within one working day, then there's a 20-minute demo where EatLabs answers a real call, and after that you get a written quote for your outlets.

The published process is three steps:

1. EatLabs calls or messages the enquirer within one working day.
2. A 20-minute demo in which EatLabs answers a real call. The demo can be given in English, Hindi or Kannada.
3. A written quote for the enquirer's outlets.

The enquiry form asks for: name, restaurant name, city, number of outlets, phone (WhatsApp), email, how they heard about EatLabs, and an optional note.

Cities offered in the form: Bengaluru, Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Hyderabad, Pune, Goa, Chennai, Kolkata, Other. Outlet-count bands: 1, 2–3, 4–10, 11+.
