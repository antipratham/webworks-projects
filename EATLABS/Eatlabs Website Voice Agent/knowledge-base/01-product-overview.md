## The core idea

> Spoken: Everything in EatLabs shares one guest record. So a call, a WhatsApp message, a booking and a bill all land on the same profile, and your host knows who is walking in.

EatLabs is sold as thirteen modules that share **one guest record**. Each module works on its own and they get better together. A restaurant turns on what it needs.

The single shared record is the reason the product hangs together: every call, message, booking and bill builds one guest profile, so the team can know that the guest on table 12 is on her tenth visit, prefers the window and is allergic to nuts — before she sits down.

## The nine booking channels

> Spoken: Bookings come in from the AI phone host, WhatsApp, Instagram, your website, Reserve with Google, Zomato, Swiggy, walk-ins and events — and they all check the same availability.

EatLabs takes bookings from: AI phone host, WhatsApp, Instagram, the restaurant's website, Reserve with Google, Zomato, Swiggy, walk-ins, and events.

All of them check **one availability engine**, so the same table cannot be taken twice.

## Languages

> Spoken: English, Hindi and Kannada — and the AI follows guests who mix them, like Hinglish.

The AI answers in **English, Hindi and Kannada**, and follows guests who mix them (Hinglish). More languages are available on request.

Languages apply across the AI phone host, WhatsApp, booking pages and the QR menu. The post-visit feedback survey runs in all three.

## Out of scope — what EatLabs is not

> Spoken: EatLabs doesn't replace your staff and it doesn't handle your money. Your team answers the phone first — the AI covers what they miss. And payments go straight to your own Razorpay account.

Three boundaries stated on the site:

- **It does not replace staff.** The team answers first; the AI covers missed calls, peak hours and closed hours.
- **Money does not pass through EatLabs.** Each restaurant connects its own Razorpay merchant account and guest payments settle directly to the restaurant's bank account.
- **Guests never need an app.** Booking, the menu, the waitlist and tickets all open in the browser.
