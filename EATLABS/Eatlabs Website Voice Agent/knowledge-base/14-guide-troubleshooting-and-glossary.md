## Common problems and where to fix them

> Spoken: Most problems are one missed setup step. Tell me what you are seeing and I can point you at the screen that fixes it.

| What you see | Why | Fix it in |
|---|---|---|
| A section says the outlet "isn't live yet" | Its address, phone, seats or opening hours are missing | Settings → Outlets |
| The floor plan is empty | No tables have been added | Reservations → Floor plan → Edit layout |
| Guests are offered no times | No tables, no shift on that day, or the booking rules rule it out | Reservations → Settings |
| A guest paid a deposit but the booking says unpaid | The Razorpay webhook was not added | Settings → Payments |
| Marketing says nothing is connected | Meta is connected but no Page is chosen for this outlet | Settings → Meta config |
| Guests are not getting confirmations | The WhatsApp messages are not approved yet, or the guest gave no number or email | Settings → WhatsApp |
| The phone line is not marked ready | No test call has been answered | AI Calls → Hunt group & routing |

## Glossary — booking and floor words

> Spoken: A cover is one guest at a meal, a no-show is a guest who booked and did not come, and a walk-in is a guest who arrives without booking.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Agenda | The list of the day's bookings. |
| Cover | One guest at a meal. A table of four is four covers. |
| Floor plan | The drawing of the tables, coloured by what is happening at each. |
| No-show | A guest who booked and did not come or cancel. |
| Shift | One service, such as lunch or dinner, with its own hours and rules. |
| Waitlist | The queue of walk-in guests waiting for a table. |
| Walk-in | A guest who arrives without booking. |

## Glossary — outlet, venue and billing words

> Spoken: An outlet is one restaurant location, and a venue is that same outlet counted for billing. Plans are priced per venue.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Outlet | One restaurant location. A group with three branches has three outlets. |
| Venue | One outlet, for billing. Plans are priced per venue. |
| Deposit | Money a guest pays when booking, to hold the table. Refunded by the restaurant's policy if they cancel. |
| POS | Point of sale — the billing system, such as Petpooja or Posist. |

## Glossary — phone and call words

> Spoken: The AI phone host answers calls your staff cannot, a hunt group is the order your staff phones ring in, and a callback is a guest waiting to be rung back.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| AI phone host | The voice assistant that answers calls when staff cannot, and books tables. |
| Callback | A guest who should be phoned back, because their call could not be completed. |
| Hunt group | The staff phones a call rings, and in what order, before the AI host answers. |

## Glossary — guest, loyalty and marketing words

> Spoken: A segment is a group of guests chosen by what they have done, a tier is a loyalty level, and consent is a guest's yes to marketing messages.

| Word | What it means |
|---|---|
| Consent | A guest's yes to receiving marketing messages. Without it they get bookings messages only. |
| CRM | Customer relationship management — here, the Guest CRM. |
| Segment | A group of guests chosen by what they have done, such as "regulars". |
| Template | A WhatsApp message Meta has approved in advance. A business must use one to message a guest first. |
| Tier | A loyalty level, such as Silver or Gold. Higher tiers earn points faster. |
