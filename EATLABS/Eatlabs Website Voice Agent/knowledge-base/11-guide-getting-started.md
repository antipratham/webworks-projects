## How a restaurant signs up

> Spoken: You book a demo, we send an invitation link, and you fill in a 7-step form that takes about 20 minutes. We check your documents and email you either way.

Sign-up is by invitation after a demo — there is no self-serve "create account" button.

1. **Book a demo.** Fill in the demo form or message EatLabs. They call back within one working day.
2. **Get your invitation.** On deciding to go ahead, an invitation link arrives by email, with a copy on WhatsApp.
3. **Fill in the restaurant's details.** A short form in **seven steps**. Progress is saved, so it can be stopped and resumed.
4. **Confirm the mobile number** with a **6-digit code** sent on WhatsApp or text message.
5. **Submit.** The EatLabs team checks the details and documents.
6. **Set a password** from the link emailed on approval.
7. **Sign in.** The restaurant is now open on the dashboard.

Most owners finish the form in one sitting, in about **20 minutes**, if the documents are ready.

## What the seven sign-up steps ask for

> Spoken: Owner details, restaurant profile, GST and tax, documents, your first outlet, account setup, then a final review.

| Step | What you enter |
|---|---|
| 1. Owner details | Full name, mobile number and email |
| 2. Restaurant profile | Brand name, type of business, registered address |
| 3. GST & tax | GSTIN, FSSAI licence number, the bank account payouts go to |
| 4. Documents | Photos or PDFs of the documents below |
| 5. First outlet | Outlet name, seats, opening hours |
| 6. Account setup | The mobile number used to sign in, confirmed by code; terms accepted here |
| 7. Review & submit | A final check, plus a promotional code if one was given |

## Documents needed to sign up

> Spoken: GST certificate, business PAN, owner photo ID, FSSAI licence and a bank letter are all required. A certificate of incorporation is needed only if you are a company.

| Document | Needed? |
|---|---|
| GST registration certificate | Required |
| Business PAN card | Required |
| Owner photo ID | Required |
| FSSAI licence | Required |
| Bank letter or cancelled cheque | Required |
| Certificate of incorporation | Only if you are a company |

Files may be PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to **10 MB each**. A clear phone photo is acceptable.

## The three answers after you submit

> Spoken: You are either approved, asked to fix something specific, or told why you were not approved. We email you either way.

- **Approved.** An email arrives with a link to set the password. The link works **once, for 15 minutes**.
- **Something needs fixing.** EatLabs says exactly what — a blurred document, a number that does not match. The same link reopens; only those items need correcting and resending.
- **Not approved.** EatLabs gives the reason, and support can be reached from the same screen.

## Sign-up links and codes that expire

> Spoken: Invitation links last 7 days, the confirmation code lasts 5 minutes, and the password link works once within 15 minutes.

| What | How long it lasts |
|---|---|
| Invitation link | 7 days — ask for a new one if it expires |
| Mobile confirmation code | 5 minutes — sent on both WhatsApp and text message |
| Password link after approval | Works once, for 15 minutes |

If the password link expires, **Forgot your password?** on the sign-in page issues a new one.

## Signing in to the dashboard

> Spoken: Sign in with your email and password in any current browser. You stay signed in on that device for up to 30 days.

- Open the dashboard in a browser, and enter email and password.
- With two-step sign-in on, enter the **6-digit code** from the authenticator app.
- Anyone belonging to more than one restaurant chooses which to open.

A session lasts up to **30 days** on that device. Supported browsers are Chrome, Edge, Safari and Firefox, kept up to date.

## Two-step sign-in and account security

> Spoken: Two-step sign-in asks for a code from your phone as well as your password. EatLabs recommends it for every owner and manager.

- Works with Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password.
- Switched on from the account's security page: scan the QR code, then enter the 6-digit code.
- **Recovery codes** are shown only once. Each works once, if the phone is lost.
- The security page lists every signed-in device and offers **Sign out all other devices** — the thing to do after losing a phone or using a shared computer.

Everyone signs in with their own account; accounts are never shared.

## What to prepare before setting up an outlet

> Spoken: Have the outlet address, the phone number guests call, the seat count, your opening hours, the staff mobile numbers and your menu ready.

- The outlet's full address, as guests should see it.
- The phone number guests call today.
- How many seats the outlet has, and how the tables are laid out.
- Opening and closing times for each day, plus lunch and dinner timings.
- The mobile numbers of the people who should answer guest calls.
- The menu — a file, a POS export, or a photo of the printed menu.

**Equipment:** a computer or tablet with a current browser, a steady internet connection at the host stand, and a printer only if table and waitlist QR codes are wanted on paper. There is no hardware to buy and nothing to install.

## Accounts a restaurant can connect

> Spoken: None of these are needed to start. Connect Razorpay for deposits, WhatsApp Business to message from your own number, and Meta or Google for marketing and reviews.

| Account | What it is for |
|---|---|
| Razorpay | Deposits and event tickets; money settles straight to the restaurant's bank |
| WhatsApp Business number | Sending from the restaurant's own number — until then EatLabs sends from its shared one |
| Facebook Page and Instagram | Campaigns, social posts and ads |
| Google Business Profile | Replying to Google reviews, and Google statistics |
| Google Ads, Google Analytics | Optional — only if already in use |

## What you wait on, and who for

> Spoken: Three things depend on someone else — the sign-up check and your calling number from EatLabs, and WhatsApp approval from Meta. Start those first.

| Waiting on | Who | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Checking your sign-up | EatLabs | They email you either way |
| Your AI calling number | EatLabs | Requested in the dashboard; EatLabs assigns it |
| WhatsApp message approval | Meta | Minutes to a few hours |
| WhatsApp display name | Meta | Up to a few days |

Start these first and do everything else while waiting.
