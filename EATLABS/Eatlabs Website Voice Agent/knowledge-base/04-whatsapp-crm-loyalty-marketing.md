## WhatsApp booking bot

> Spoken: Guests book, change and cancel in plain language on WhatsApp, Instagram or SMS. The AI answers in English, Hindi and Kannada, and copes when people mix them.

Guests book, change and cancel in plain language on WhatsApp, Instagram or SMS — and by voice on the phone line. The AI answers in English, Hindi and Kannada, and follows guests who mix them (Hinglish).

A worked example from the site: a guest asks "Table for 4 this Saturday around 8?", the bot offers window or terrace, the guest replies in mixed Hindi and English asking for a birthday cake, and the booking is confirmed with the cake noted — replied in 2 seconds, with no staff needed.

## Shared team inbox

> Spoken: One thread per guest, shared across the team. Staff can take over from the bot at any point, and they get suggested replies.

- **One thread per guest**, shared across the team.
- **Staff take-over** from the AI bot mid-conversation.
- **Suggested replies** for staff.
- Guests can type "points" in any of the three languages to see their loyalty balance and available rewards.

## Your own WhatsApp number

> Spoken: You can use your own WhatsApp Business number, or start on the EatLabs shared number.

A restaurant can connect **its own WhatsApp Business number**, or start on **EatLabs' shared number**.

Sending runs through the **Meta WhatsApp Cloud API** on the restaurant's own WhatsApp Business Account, under Meta's policies. Templates need Meta's approval, and EatLabs cannot guarantee approval or its timing.

## Consent and opt-out

> Spoken: Only guests who opted in get marketing, and consent is checked again at the moment of sending. A guest can reply STOP any time and it takes effect across every channel at once.

- Built-in consent handling: guests can reply **STOP** at any time.
- Marketing goes only to guests who opted in, and **consent is checked again at send time**.
- Every marketing message carries a **one-tap opt-out**, and withdrawal propagates to every channel immediately. Restaurants must not route around it.
- **Transactional messages** — booking confirmations, reminders, deposit links, ticket deliveries — are sent on the strength of the guest's own interaction and are not marketing.

Compliance with TRAI's commercial communication rules, DLT registration for SMS sender IDs and headers, and Meta's WhatsApp Business policies is the **restaurant's** responsibility. EatLabs enforces recorded consent in code but cannot know whether that consent was properly obtained.

## Guest CRM

> Spoken: One profile per guest for the whole group, with a timeline of every call, booking, visit, bill, review and loyalty event.

One profile per guest across every channel and outlet.

- **Timeline:** calls, bookings, visits, bills, feedback, events and loyalty.
- **Spend tracking:** lifetime spend, average bill and preferred outlet, calculated from POS bills.
- **Preferences saved automatically** from calls, chats and past visits, and shown to the host at seating — seating preference, dietary and allergy flags, occasions, usual order.
- **Birthday and anniversary reminders.**
- **Segments** such as "Indiranagar regulars who have never tried Koramangala".
- Shared across every outlet in the group, with **permissions per outlet**.

## Loyalty, vouchers and offers

> Spoken: Points per hundred rupees spent, plus points for visits, referrals, feedback and birthdays. Points earned at one outlet can be spent at any of them.

- Points per ₹100 spent, plus visit, referral, feedback and birthday points.
- **Rewards and tiers:** ₹-off or free-item rewards, tiers with earn multipliers, expiry rules the restaurant sets.
- **Group-wide:** points earned at one outlet can be spent at any outlet.
- Digital loyalty card by QR, which also works as a printed sticker.
- Automatic reminders before points expire, on tier upgrades, on birthdays, and win-back messages.
- Vouchers guests can buy online.
- Partner offers from banks, wallets and brands, with monthly settlement statements.
- **Cross-brand promotion:** partner with another restaurant to reach each other's guests **without either side ever seeing the other's guest data**.

## WhatsApp campaigns

> Spoken: You send approved templates to any guest segment, now or scheduled, and the report shows how many bookings it actually produced within seven days.

- Send approved templates to any guest segment, immediately or scheduled.
- Segments come straight from the Guest CRM — regulars, lapsed guests, birthdays this month.
- **Reports:** sent, delivered, read, replied, **plus bookings generated within 7 days**.

Deliverability depends on the carrier and on Meta. Delivery is not guaranteed, and credits spent on an accepted send are not returned if the recipient's device never shows it.

## Attribution and tracking

> Spoken: Every booking page and WhatsApp link carries your tracking, so you can see which ad or post actually filled tables — not just which got clicks.

Every booking page and WhatsApp link carries the restaurant's tracking, via **GA4, Meta Pixel and GTM**.

The bookings-by-source report shows the funnel: ad clicks → booking page → booked → visited, broken down by Instagram ad, Google search, WhatsApp broadcast, website, Meta ad and Zomato.

Exports: bookings, guests, points and campaign results as **CSV**, or a daily summary on WhatsApp.
