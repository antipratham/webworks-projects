## The published plans

> Spoken: There are three plans. A seven-day free trial at no cost, Growth at thirty-five thousand rupees per month per venue, and Enterprise with custom pricing for groups. GST is extra.

Simple monthly pricing **per venue**. The site states **no setup fee and no contract**.

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Free Trial** | ₹0 for 7 days | Trying the full Growth platform |
| **Growth** (Recommended) | ₹35,000 / month / venue | Complete AI restaurant operations |
| **Enterprise** | Custom | Restaurant groups and chains |

Prices are in **INR and exclusive of GST**. WhatsApp messages are billed at Meta's rate.

## What Enterprise means

> Spoken: Enterprise is for restaurant groups and chains — custom limits, more outlets, a dedicated number pool and priority support, on terms set for you.

For restaurant groups and chains: custom limits, more outlets, a dedicated number pool, and priority support, on terms set individually. Pricing is quoted, not published. The CTA is "Talk to us".

## Credits: the usage currency

> Spoken: On top of the plan fee, metered usage runs on credits. Voice minutes, marketing messages, AI usage and storage all draw from one pool, so you're never stuck holding spare messages and no minutes.

**This is in the Terms of Service, not on the pricing page.** Treat it as accurate but confirm specifics with sales.

A plan grants a number of **Credits** each billing period. Everything metered spends from that one pool:

| Meter | Unit | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| Voice minutes | each started minute | The carrier leg and the AI answering engine |
| Marketing messages | each template send | WhatsApp marketing template rates |
| AI credits | credit | AI host, AI replies, AI Studio, other model usage |
| Storage | GB, charged as a level | Media files and call recordings held |

The stated rationale: one pool means you are never left holding unused messages and no minutes.

## How credits expire

> Spoken: Plan credits reset each period and don't bank. Credits you bought carry forward. Spending always uses the free plan credits first.

| Bucket | Source | At period end |
|---|---|---|
| **Plan Credits** | The plan's periodic grant | Replaced. Unused ones do **not** bank |
| **Purchased Credits** | Top-ups bought, or credits granted | **Carried forward** |

Spending always draws on Plan Credits first, so you never burn credits you paid for while free ones expire beside them.

Credits are **not refundable**, have no cash value, and are not transferable between accounts.

## Running out of credits

> Spoken: If credits hit zero, metered features pause until you top up — but your data, exports and any bookings already taken are completely unaffected.

If Credits reach zero, **metered features stop until topped up**. Non-metered features, the restaurant's data, and the ability to export it are unaffected. EatLabs warns before that point.

**Bookings already taken are never cancelled because of a credit balance.**

## Limits are separate from credits

> Spoken: Things like outlet count, phone numbers, staff seats and concurrent AI calls are fixed plan limits. Credits don't raise them — changing a limit means changing plan.

Fixed limits on a plan, which Credits do **not** raise: outlets, phone numbers, Staff User seats, connected social channels, concurrent AI calls, call recording retention, storage ceilings. Changing a limit means changing plan.

## Invoicing and payment terms

> Spoken: Fees are in rupees plus GST, payable in advance each period, and invoices are due on receipt.

- Fees in INR, **exclusive of GST**.
- Subscription fees payable **in advance** each period. Top-ups payable on purchase.
- Invoices go to the billing details on the account. Keep GSTIN and address current — a tax invoice cannot be reissued to correct wrong details after that period's return is filed.
- Invoices due on receipt unless the order form says otherwise.
- **Interest of 1.5% per month** may be charged on amounts more than 30 days overdue, and the platform may be suspended after **15 days' written notice** of non-payment.
- **Price changes** take effect at next renewal with at least **30 days' notice**. A restaurant that does not accept a price change may decline to renew.

## Term, renewal and cancellation

> Spoken: The subscription renews automatically for the same period unless either side gives 30 days' notice before it ends.

- The agreement runs for the subscription period on the order form and **renews automatically** for the same period unless either side gives notice at least **30 days** before the period ends.
- **Termination for cause:** either side, on a material breach not fixed within 30 days of written notice.
- **Termination for convenience by EatLabs:** 60 days' written notice, with a refund of unused Purchased Credits and the unused part of any prepaid period.
- A restaurant may stop renewing at any time under the 30-day notice rule.

⚠️ **Note the tension:** the pricing page says "no contract", while the Terms describe an auto-renewing subscription period with 30 days' notice to exit. See the guardrails file — do not assert "no contract, cancel anytime" without qualification.

## What happens to data on termination

> Spoken: When you leave, the dashboard stays open in export mode, you have 30 days to export everything as JSON and CSV, and live data is deleted at 60 days.

| When | What |
|---|---|
| Immediately | Metered features stop; dashboard stays available in read-and-export mode |
| For 30 days after | Export Guest Data and records as JSON and CSV; EatLabs will help |
| After 60 days | Tenant data deleted from live systems |
| Up to 90 days | Encrypted backups age out on their own schedule |
| As law requires | Payment, ticket and tax records (8 years for GST) and audit records |

Deletion can be requested sooner in writing — but once deleted, Guest Data cannot be recovered, so export first.
