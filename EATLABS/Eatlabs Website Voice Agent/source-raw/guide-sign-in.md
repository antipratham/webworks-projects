How you, your managers and your team get into the dashboard, and how to keep the account safe.

### Signing in

- Open the dashboard in your browser.
- Enter your email and password.
- If you have two-step sign-in switched on, enter the 6-digit code from your authenticator app.
- If you belong to more than one restaurant, choose which one to open.
You stay signed in on that device for up to 30 days. Use Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, kept up to date.

### Forgot your password

- On the sign-in page, press Forgot your password?
- Enter the email you sign in with.
- Open the email we send and set a new password. Then sign in with it.

### Two-step sign-in

Two-step sign-in asks for a code from your phone as well as your password, so a stolen password alone is not enough. We recommend it for every owner and manager.
- Install an authenticator app on your phone — Google Authenticator, Microsoft Authenticator or 1Password all work.
- In the dashboard, open your account’s security page and switch on two-step sign-in.
- Scan the QR code with the app, then enter the 6-digit code it shows.
- Save your recovery codes somewhere safe. Each one works once if you lose your phone, and they are only shown once.

### Your team’s accounts

Everyone signs in with their own account — never share yours. You invite people from Settings → Team & roles; they get an email (and a WhatsApp message, if you add their mobile), set a password and sign in. Each person only sees what their role allows. See Team and outlets.

### Left signed in somewhere?

On your account’s security page you can see every device you are signed in on, and press Sign out all other devices. Do this if you lose a phone or signed in on a shared computer.
