## What you need before you start

Keep these ready. Looking for them halfway through a form is what turns twenty minutes into an afternoon.

### Documents for sign-up

| Document
| Needed?
| GST registration certificate
| Required
| Business PAN card
| Required
| Owner photo ID
| Required
| FSSAI licence
| Required
| Bank letter or cancelled cheque
| Required
| Certificate of incorporation
| Only if you are a company
PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each.

### Details for setting up your outlet

- The outlet’s full address, as guests should see it.
- The phone number guests call today.
- How many seats the outlet has, and how the tables are laid out.
- Opening and closing times for each day, and your lunch and dinner timings.
- The mobile numbers of the people who should answer guest calls.
- Your menu — a file, a POS export or just a photo of the printed menu.

### Accounts to connect

None of these are needed to start. Connect each when you want what it gives you.
| Account
| What it is for
| Razorpay
| Taking deposits and selling event tickets. The money goes straight to your bank.
| WhatsApp Business number
| Sending messages from your own number. Until then we send from ours.
| Facebook Page and Instagram
| Campaigns, social posts and ads.
| Google Business Profile
| Replying to Google reviews and seeing your Google stats.
| Google Ads, Google Analytics
| Optional — only if you already use them.

### Equipment

- A computer or tablet with an up-to-date browser — Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.
- A steady internet connection at the host stand.
- A printer, if you want table QR codes and waitlist QR codes on paper.
There is no hardware to buy and nothing to install.
