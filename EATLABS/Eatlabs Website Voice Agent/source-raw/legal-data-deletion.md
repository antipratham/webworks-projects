### 2.What to tell us

Four things, and the request can be acted on straight away:
- The phone number you used with the restaurant. This is how the record is found — it is the key for a diner’s data across bookings, calls, messages and loyalty.
- The restaurant’s name (and the outlet, if you know which one). Each restaurant’s data is separate, so a request can be for one restaurant or for every restaurant on EatLabs that holds you. Say which you want.
- What you want deleted — everything, or just a part of it: marketing consent, a call recording, a dietary note, a review reply.
- An email address or number we can reply on.
You do not have to give a reason. We will not ask for one.

### 3.How we check it is you

We send a one-time code to the phone number on the record, and you read it back to us or reply with it. If that number no longer works, we will find another way — a deposit payment reference, the date and time of a specific booking, or the email the confirmation went to.
We will not delete on an unverified request. That is a protection for you, not an obstacle: your phone number is not a secret, and without a check anyone who knew it could erase your bookings, your loyalty points or your table tonight.

It is not a single row being removed and the rest left behind.

### 5.What is deleted, and what has to stay

| Data
| What happens
| Your guest profile — name, phone, email, dietary notes, allergies, occasions
| Deleted outright
| Call recordings
| The audio and every version of it deleted
| Call transcripts
| Deleted
| Your number on calls and messages
| Removed; delivery counts stay as numbers with nobody attached
| Bookings and visits
| Your name and number stripped. The row stays as an anonymous cover, so the restaurant’s history of how busy a night was does not change
| Loyalty balance and redemptions
| Deleted. An unredeemed balance goes with it
| Campaign and automation records
| Your entries deleted; the campaign’s reported reach is left as it was
| Reviews you posted publicly on Google or Zomato
| We tombstone our copy. The public review belongs to that platform and must be removed there
| Payment, refund and ticket records
| Kept, with your personal fields stripped to the legal minimum. Indian tax law requires these for 8 years
| Audit record of the deletion itself
| Kept — and it holds no personal data about you. It exists so that if you ever ask “did you actually delete me?”, the answer can be proved
The DPDP Act allows personal data to be retained where another law requires it. Where that applies, we strip everything not legally required and record the basis for what remains.
Aggregate figures — covers served, revenue by night, dish popularity — never identified you and are not reversible into you. They stay.

### 6.If you only want the messages to stop

Deletion is a big step, and often not what is actually wanted. If the problem is marketing:
- Tap the opt-out button on any marketing WhatsApp message, or reply STOP to a marketing SMS.
- It takes effect immediately and across every channel at once — WhatsApp, SMS and email.
- Your bookings, loyalty points and visit history stay exactly as they are.
- You will still get messages about a booking you have actually made. Those are not marketing.
You can also ask us to withdraw consent on your behalf at privacy@eatlabs.io.

### 8.If you are a restaurant on EatLabs

Three different requests, so say which one you mean:
A guest has asked you to delete them. Raise it from the dashboard, or forward their request to privacy@eatlabs.io. You remain the Data Fiduciary — the instruction is yours, and we carry it out across every store within seven days and send you the confirmation to keep.
You want a staff member removed. Deactivate the login in the dashboard yourself. Their name stays on the audit entries for actions they took, because an audit trail with the actor removed is not an audit trail. Ask us if you need their contact details erased as well.
You are closing your account. Export everything first — the dashboard exports to JSON and CSV, and we will help. Then:

Ask and we will delete sooner. Once it is gone it cannot be restored, so export first.

### 9.Disconnecting a linked account

Removing an app connection is not the same as deleting data, so do both if that is what you want.
- WhatsApp, Facebook or Instagram: disconnect the channel in the dashboard, and remove EatLabs from your Meta Business settings. Tokens held for that connection are deleted when you disconnect.
- Google Business Profile or Analytics: disconnect in the dashboard, and revoke access in your Google account’s third-party app permissions.
- Razorpay or your POS: disconnect in the dashboard. We delete the stored keys.
Messages already sent through Meta’s platform are governed by Meta’s own retention, and a public review on Google stays Google’s until it is removed there.
