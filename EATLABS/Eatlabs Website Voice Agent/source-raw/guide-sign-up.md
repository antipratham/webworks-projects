From your first call with us to your restaurant open on the dashboard. Most owners finish the form in one sitting, in about 20 minutes, if they have their documents ready.

### The steps

- Book a demo. Fill in the demo form or message us. We call you back within one working day.
- Get your invitation. When you decide to go ahead, we send an invitation link to your email, and a copy on WhatsApp.
- Fill in your restaurant’s details. The link opens a short form in seven steps. Your progress is saved as you go, so you can stop and come back.
- Confirm your mobile number with a 6-digit code we send on WhatsApp or by text message.
- Submit. Our team checks your details and documents.
- Set your password. Once you are approved, we email you a link to set your password.
- Sign in. Your restaurant is open on the dashboard. Next: Open your outlet.

### What the form asks for

| Step
| What you enter
| 1. Owner details
| Your full name, mobile number and email.
| 2. Restaurant profile
| Your brand name, type of business and registered address.
| 3. GST & tax
| Your GSTIN, FSSAI licence number and the bank account payouts go to.
| 4. Documents
| Photos or PDFs of your documents. See What you need.
| 5. First outlet
| The outlet’s name, how many it seats and its opening hours.
| 6. Account setup
| The mobile number you will sign in with, confirmed with a code. You also accept the terms here.
| 7. Review & submit
| Check everything once. If we gave you a promotional code, enter it here.
Documents can be PDF, JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 10 MB each. A clear phone photo is fine.

### After you submit

We check your details and documents, and email you either way. There are three possible answers:
- Approved. We email you a link to set your password. The link works once, for 15 minutes, so use it when you get it.
- Something needs fixing. We tell you exactly what — a blurred document, a number that does not match. Open the same link, fix only those things and send it back.
- Not approved. We tell you why. You can chat with our support team from the same screen.

### If something goes wrong

- “This invitation link has expired.” Invitation links last 7 days. Ask us for a new one.
- The code did not arrive. It goes out on both WhatsApp and text message and lasts 5 minutes. Wait a minute, then ask for a new code.
- The password link has expired. Use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page to get a new one.
