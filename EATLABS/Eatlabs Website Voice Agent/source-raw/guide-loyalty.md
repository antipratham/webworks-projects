## Loyalty, offers and vouchers

Reward guests for coming back, and keep them coming back, across every outlet you own.

### Points and rewards

- Guests earn points for every ₹100 they spend, plus points for visits, referrals, feedback and birthdays.
- They spend points on rewards you choose — money off, or a free dish.
- Tiers give your best guests more points per visit.
- Points earned at one outlet can be spent at any of your outlets.
Set it up in Loyalty → Points & rules and Loyalty → Tiers.

### What guests see

A digital loyalty card they open from a QR code (which also works as a printed sticker), and automatic messages before points expire, on a tier upgrade and on their birthday. They can also type “points” on WhatsApp to see their balance.

### Offers and vouchers

Loyalty → Offers & vouchers is where you create offers and vouchers guests can buy online — a gift voucher, a dinner-for-two deal.
