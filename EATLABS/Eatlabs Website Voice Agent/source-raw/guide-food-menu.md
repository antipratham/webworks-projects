## Food menu and QR ordering

A menu guests open by scanning the QR code on their table — no app to download, in English, Hindi and Kannada.

### Adding dishes

- Food Menu → Add item for one dish at a time.
- Food Menu → Import from a file or an export from Petpooja, Posist or another POS.
- Translate the menu into Hindi and Kannada with AI.
For each dish, mark it veg or non-veg and add its diet labels — vegan, Jain, no dairy, gluten-free, no nuts, mild — and its allergens.

### What guests see

- Filters for their diet, with the familiar green and red veg dots.
- Badges like “Most ordered this week”, bestseller and chef’s special. Arrange these in Food Menu → Tags & ranking.
- An AI food concierge they can ask, for example, “something not spicy, veg, under ₹400”.
The concierge never suggests a dish to a guest avoiding an allergen unless your kitchen has confirmed that dish’s allergens.

### Ordering from the table

Everyone at a table can order from their own phone, and your staff can order for guests without one. The table still gets one bill and one payment. Print each table’s QR code from Food Menu → Table QR codes.
