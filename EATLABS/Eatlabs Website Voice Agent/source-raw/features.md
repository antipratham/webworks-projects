### Guest CRM

One profile per guest for your whole restaurant group.
- Timeline: calls, bookings, visits, bills, feedback, events and loyalty.
- Spend tracking: lifetime spend, average bill and preferred outlet, calculated from POS bills.
- Segments such as “Indiranagar regulars who have never tried Koramangala”.
- Shared across every outlet in the group, with permissions per outlet.

### AI menu and QR dining

Table QR menu with no app to download, in English, Hindi and Kannada.
- Diet filters: veg, vegan, Jain, no dairy, gluten-free, no nuts and mild, with the Indian veg/non-veg dot convention.
- AI food concierge: guests can ask “something not spicy, veg, under ₹400”. Diet and allergy rules are checked in code on every suggestion; dishes whose allergens the kitchen hasn’t confirmed are never suggested to guests avoiding an allergen.
- Badges: “Most ordered this week”, bestseller, trending and chef’s special.
- Shared table sessions: everyone at the table orders from their own phone, and staff order for guests without a phone. The table gets one bill and one payment.
- Import your menu from a Petpooja, Posist or other POS export, and translate it into Hindi and Kannada with AI.

### Loyalty, vouchers and offers

Points per ₹100 spent, plus visit, referral, feedback and birthday points.
- Rewards and tiers: ₹-off or free-item rewards, tiers with earn multipliers, and expiry rules you set.
- Group-wide programme: points earned at one outlet can be spent at any of them.
- Digital loyalty card by QR, which also works as a printed sticker.
- Automatic reminders before points expire, on tier upgrades, on birthdays, and win-back messages.
- Vouchers guests can buy online.
- Partner offers from banks, wallets and brands, with monthly settlement statements.
- Cross-brand promotion: partner with another restaurant to reach each other’s guests without either of you ever sharing guest data.

### WhatsApp campaigns

Send approved templates to any guest segment, now or at a scheduled time.
- Only guests who opted in receive messages, and consent is checked again at send time.
- Reports: sent, delivered, read and replied, plus bookings generated within 7 days.
- Segments come straight from the Guest CRM — regulars, lapsed guests, birthdays this month.

Ticketed events, one-off or weekly, using the full venue, part of it or shared seating.

### Multi-outlet

Manage the whole group from one account.

- Set per outlet: phone numbers, floor plans, menus and hours.
- Group events at several outlets, and offers that promote a sister outlet.
