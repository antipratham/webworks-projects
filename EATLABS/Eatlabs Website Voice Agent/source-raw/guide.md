## How EatLabs works

EatLabs runs the guest side of your restaurant in one place: the phone, WhatsApp, bookings, the floor, the menu, loyalty, marketing and reviews. This guide walks through all of it, step by step, in plain language.

### What EatLabs does

Most restaurants use one app for bookings, another for WhatsApp, a notebook for the phone and a spreadsheet for regulars. EatLabs replaces them with one system. Every call, message, booking and bill adds to one record per guest, so your team always knows who is coming and what they like.
- Answers your phone. Your staff get the call first; when nobody picks up, or you are closed, the AI phone host answers and books the table.
- Takes bookings everywhere. Phone, WhatsApp, Instagram, your website, Google, Zomato, Swiggy and walk-ins all land in one agenda, and a table can never be double-booked.
- Runs the floor. A live floor plan shows which tables are free, seated or running late, plus a waitlist guests join by scanning a QR code.
- Brings guests back. Loyalty points, offers, WhatsApp campaigns and birthday messages, sent only to guests who said yes.
- Looks after your reputation. Google reviews in one inbox, AI-drafted replies, and private feedback after every visit.
- Shows you the numbers. Covers, no-shows, where bookings come from and which campaigns paid for themselves.
It all works in English, Hindi and Kannada.

### A booking, from start to finish

This is what happens on a normal evening, with nobody pressing anything:
- Priya calls your restaurant at 6 pm. Your staff are busy, so the AI phone host answers, checks which tables are free and books her a table for four at 8 pm.
- The booking appears on your agenda straight away, and Priya gets a confirmation on WhatsApp.
- Two hours before, she gets a reminder with Confirm and Cancel buttons. She confirms.
- She arrives; your host sees her on the floor plan, with a note that she is vegetarian and celebrating an anniversary.
- After the meal she is asked for feedback on WhatsApp, and earns loyalty points on her bill.
- A month later she is in your "regulars" segment, and gets your next offer.
