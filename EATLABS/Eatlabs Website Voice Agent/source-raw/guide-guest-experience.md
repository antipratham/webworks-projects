- For your guests
- What your guests see
Everything your diners do happens on their own phone, from a link or a QR code. There is no app to download and no account to create.

### Booking a table

Guests book by calling you, by messaging you on WhatsApp or Instagram, through Google, or on your booking page — a link you put on your website, Instagram bio and Google profile. On the booking page they:
- Choose the date, time and number of guests.
- Enter their name and mobile number.
- Pay a deposit, if you ask for one, by UPI or card.
- Get a confirmation on WhatsApp with a link to their booking.
From that link they can check, or cancel, their booking at any time before they arrive. Deposit refunds follow your policy automatically.

### Joining the waitlist

At the door they scan your waitlist QR code, enter their name and party size, and see their place in the queue update live. When the table is ready, they get a WhatsApp message.

### At the table

They scan the QR code on the table to open your menu, in English, Hindi or Kannada. They can filter for their diet, ask the AI food concierge for ideas, and order from their phone. The whole table shares one bill.

### Events and tickets

From your event link they choose tickets and extras, pay by UPI or card, and get their ticket on WhatsApp. At the door, your team scans the QR code on it.

### Loyalty and vouchers

Their loyalty card opens from a QR code and shows their points and rewards. They can also buy vouchers from you online, and type “points” on WhatsApp to see their balance.

### After the visit

They get a short feedback request on WhatsApp. Happy guests are invited to leave a Google review; unhappy ones reach you privately first.

### Their privacy

Guest data stays in India. Guests only get marketing if they agreed to, can reply STOP at any time, and can ask for their data to be deleted. The full details are in the Guest Privacy Notice.
