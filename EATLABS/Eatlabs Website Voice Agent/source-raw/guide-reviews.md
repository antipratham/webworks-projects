## Reviews and feedback

Hear from guests before Google does, and reply to every review without writing each one yourself.

### One inbox for reviews

Reviews → Inbox collects your Google reviews (checked every 15 minutes) and imported Zomato reviews. Each is tagged by what it is about — food, service, ambience, price or hygiene.

### AI-drafted replies

For each review, EatLabs drafts a reply in your restaurant’s voice and in the reviewer’s language. You read it, change anything you like, and publish. Replies post to Google; Zomato does not allow replies from outside its app.

### Private feedback

After every visit, guests are asked for feedback on WhatsApp. A low score comes to you privately and straight away — in Reviews → Private feedback — so you can put things right before it becomes a public review.

### The weekly digest

Every Monday you get a short summary, for example: “Service complaints up 33%, mostly Friday. Adjust the Friday roster.”
