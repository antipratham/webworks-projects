The words this guide and the dashboard use, in plain language.

### Terms

| Word
| What it means
| Agenda
| The list of the day’s bookings.
| AI phone host
| The voice assistant that answers your calls when your staff cannot, and books tables.
| Callback
| A guest who should be phoned back, because their call could not be completed.
| Consent
| A guest’s yes to receiving marketing messages. Without it they get bookings messages only.
| Cover
| One guest at a meal. A table of four is four covers.
| CRM
| Customer relationship management — here, the Guest CRM: your list of guests and everything about them.
| Deposit
| Money a guest pays when booking, to hold the table. Refunded by your policy if they cancel.
| Floor plan
| The drawing of your tables, coloured by what is happening at each.
| Hunt group
| The staff phones a call rings, and in what order, before the AI host answers.
| No-show
| A guest who booked and did not come or cancel.
| Outlet
| One restaurant location. A group with three branches has three outlets.
| POS
| Point of sale — your billing system, such as Petpooja or Posist.
| Segment
| A group of guests chosen by what they have done, such as “regulars” or “not visited in 60 days”.
| Shift
| One service, such as lunch or dinner, with its own hours and rules.
| Template
| A WhatsApp message Meta has approved in advance. Businesses must use one to message a guest first.
| Tier
| A loyalty level, such as Silver or Gold. Higher tiers earn points faster.
| Venue
| One outlet, for billing. Plans are priced per venue.
| Waitlist
| The queue of walk-in guests waiting for a table.
| Walk-in
| A guest who arrives without booking.
| Webhook
| An automatic message one system sends another — Razorpay uses one to tell EatLabs a guest has paid.
