You booked a table, scanned a menu or called a restaurant that runs on EatLabs. Here is what is held about you, who can see it, how long it stays, and how to have it deleted.

### 1.The short version

- The restaurant you contacted holds your details. EatLabs is the software it uses — we hold the data on its behalf and do nothing else with it.
- We never share your details with another restaurant. Not for promotions, not ever.
- Your data stays on servers in India.
- If a call was recorded, you were told at the start, and the recording is deleted after 90 days.
- You can be deleted. Ask here and it is done within seven days.
- We never see your card or UPI details.

### 2.Who holds your data

### 4.What it is used for

The restaurant uses it to run the restaurant:
- to hold and confirm your booking, and to tell you if something changes;
- to seat you, remember your table preference, and get your order right;
- to keep you safe around allergens — a dietary note you give is attached to your booking so the kitchen and your server see it;
- to take a deposit or a ticket payment, and to refund it under the restaurant’s policy;
- to run its loyalty programme, if you joined one;
- to ask for feedback after a visit, and to reply to a review;
- to understand its own business in aggregate — how busy a Friday is, which dishes sell.
Marketing is separate. Offers, campaigns and festival messages are only sent if you gave marketing consent. The system will not send them without it. Transactional messages about a booking you actually made are not marketing, and you will keep getting those while a booking is live.

### 5.Phone calls and the AI host

The restaurant’s phone may be answered by an AI host, in English, Hindi or Kannada.
- You can always ask for a person. Say so and the call is transferred to the team, with a one-line summary of what you have already said, so you do not have to repeat yourself. If nobody is free, a callback is logged.
- Recording is only on if the restaurant switched it on, and if it is, you are told at the start of the call, before the conversation.
- A recording is kept for 90 days and then deleted. The transcript is kept for 90 days.
- Transcripts are automatically cleaned before they are stored: card numbers, Aadhaar-like numbers and spoken codes are stripped out. Do not read a card number or an OTP out on the call — no restaurant needs it, and the only reliable protection is not to say it.
- The AI can check availability and take or change your own booking. It cannot touch anyone else’s booking, and it cannot issue refunds.

### 6.WhatsApp, SMS and email

- Booking confirmations, reminders, deposit links, tickets and “your table is ready” come through WhatsApp, with SMS as a fallback.
- WhatsApp messages are delivered through Meta’s WhatsApp Business Platform, so Meta processes your number and the message content in order to deliver it, under its own terms.
- To stop marketing: tap the opt-out button on any marketing message, or reply STOP to an SMS. It takes effect immediately and across every channel — WhatsApp, SMS and email at once. You do not have to ask twice, and you will still get messages about a booking you have made.

### 7.Payments

You pay on Razorpay’s own payment page, on the restaurant’s merchant account. The money goes from you to the restaurant.
Card numbers, CVVs and UPI PINs never reach EatLabs or the restaurant’s dashboard. What is stored is the amount, the method, the status and a reference, so the restaurant can confirm your deposit and refund it if it needs to.

### 8.Who can see your details

- The restaurant you dealt with — its staff, with the permissions their role gives them. If the restaurant is a group, outlets in that same group may see you as a shared guest.
- No other restaurant. Ever. Isolation between restaurants is enforced in the database itself. When two brands run a joint promotion, each one reaches the other’s guests without either seeing the other’s list.
- EatLabs staff only when support needs it. That requires a time-limited session opened with a reason and a ticket number; everything read in it is logged to a record that cannot be altered; and the restaurant’s owner is notified that it happened.
- Service providers that make the thing work: Amazon Web Services (hosting, in Mumbai), Meta (WhatsApp), Razorpay (payments), the phone carrier, and the AI model providers for the part of a conversation they are asked to answer. The Privacy Policy lists each one.
- A court, regulator or police authority acting under lawful authority.

### 9.Where it is stored, and what leaves India

Your data is stored in India, in Amazon’s Mumbai region. It is not copied to another region.
Three things cross the border, and only these: the text of a conversation sent to an AI model provider to generate a reply; a WhatsApp message passing through Meta’s platform to be delivered; and public review content read back from Google or Zomato. Each is covered by a contract with that provider.

### 11.Your rights, and how to use them

Under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you can ask for:
- A copy of what is held about you, and what it is used for.
- A correction — a misspelt name, an old number, a dietary note that is no longer right.
- Deletion of your personal data.
- Withdrawal of consent for marketing, at any time.
- A nominee who can exercise these rights for you if you die or cannot act for yourself.
- A complaint, to our Grievance Officer and then to the Data Protection Board of India.
All of them start the same way: [the data deletion and rights page](/legal/data-deletion). It tells you what to send, how we check it is really you, and how long it takes. Or write straight to privacy@eatlabs.io.
We confirm your identity before we act — normally a one-time code to the number we hold. That is there to protect you: without it, anyone who knows your number could delete your bookings.

### 12.Decisions made automatically

Nothing about you is decided solely by a machine in a way that affects your legal rights. The AI host books tables; it does not rank or score you. Restaurants do build marketing segments — “visited in the last 90 days”, “likes the tasting menu” — and those decide whether you receive an offer, nothing more. Marketing consent is checked before anything is sent.

### 13.Children

The guest app is not meant for children under 18 to use on their own. A restaurant must not use EatLabs to track or advertise to a child. If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child’s details are held here, write to privacy@eatlabs.io and we will remove them.
