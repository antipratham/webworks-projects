## Built for the restaurants that lose the most to a ringing phone.

Whether you run one busy room or a group across the city, EatLabs fits the way you already work.

### Busy single restaurants

that lose bookings to unanswered phones.
Your phone rings during service and nobody can pick up. EatLabs answers, books the table and tells your host what happened — you keep every booking.
- AI host answers when staff can’t, 24 × 7
- Missed-call recovery on WhatsApp
- One live agenda for phone, WhatsApp and walk-ins
- Live in a day, no code needed
See how it works for you

### Restaurant groups

that want one guest database and one loyalty programme.
A guest who eats at Indiranagar should be recognised in Koramangala. EatLabs keeps one profile per guest and one points balance across every outlet.
- One guest record across all outlets
- Group-wide points, tiers and rewards
- Per-outlet numbers, floor plans, menus and hours
- Roles: owner, outlet manager, host, marketing, read-only
See how it works for you

### Bars and venues

that run events, gigs and ticketed nights.
Sell tickets on WhatsApp, check guests in by QR, and never double-book a table that an event already holds.
- Ticketed events, one-off or weekly
- UPI payment, e-tickets and QR check-in at the door
- Add-ons: drinks packages, table upgrades
- Tables blocked automatically — no clashes with bookings
See how it works for you

that want to own their guest relationships.
Aggregator guests become your guests. Import their bookings, see what commission really costs you, and bring them back direct.
- Aggregator bookings in your agenda, duplicates detected
- Leakage report: commission, fees and discounts you fund
- Channel split: walk-in, direct, corporate, event, aggregator
- Loyalty and WhatsApp campaigns to win them back direct
