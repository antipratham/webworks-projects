## Insights and reports

The numbers behind your restaurant, without a spreadsheet.

### What you can see

| Report
| What it tells you
| Overview
| Covers, bookings by where they came from, no-show rate, calls, and new vs returning guests.
| Covers & bookings
| How busy each day and shift was, and the trend.
| No-shows
| Who does not turn up, when, and how much it costs you.
| New vs returning
| Whether guests come back, and how often.
| Dishes
| Most ordered, highest margin and rising dishes.
| Operations
| Table turns, time at the table, shift performance and your busiest hours.
You also get a short weekly AI summary of how the business did, and, in Marketing, the revenue each campaign brought in.
