- Guest CRM
One profile for every guest, built automatically from their calls, messages, bookings, bills and feedback — shared across all your outlets.

### What a guest profile shows

- A timeline of every call, booking, visit, bill, review, event ticket and loyalty point.
- Lifetime spend, average bill and favourite outlet, worked out from POS bills.
- How often they turn up, cancel or do not show.
- Preferences your team adds — window seat, vegetarian, nut allergy, anniversary date, their usual drink.
Your host sees these notes on the booking, so the guest is recognised the moment they arrive.

### Segments

Guest CRM → Segments groups guests by what they have done — regulars, guests who have not visited in two months, birthdays this month. Use a segment as the audience for a campaign or an offer.

### Consent

Marketing messages only go to guests who agreed to receive them, and any guest can reply STOP at any time. EatLabs keeps track of this for you, and checks again just before each message is sent.
