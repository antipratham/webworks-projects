The first thing to do after you sign in. It takes about 15 minutes, and nothing else works until it is done.

### Your checklist

Settings → Settings dashboard shows every setup step and ticks each one off as you finish it. Keep it open in another tab while you work through these pages.

### 1. Outlet basics

Go to Settings → Outlets → Add outlet. One form asks for:
- The outlet’s name and a short code (for example IND for Indiranagar).
- Its address, and the phone number guests call.
- How many seats it has.
- Its opening hours (see below).
- The people who answer guest calls.
- Where the menu comes from — you can copy it from another of your outlets.
Only the address, phone number and seats are required to open. The map pin is optional — add it later.

### 2. Opening hours

In the same form. At least one day must be open — guests can only book times when you are open. You can give two windows a day if you close between lunch and dinner.
Separate lunch and dinner services, each with its own timings, are called shifts. You set those on the next page.
Your outlet is live as soon as you save. There is no approval to wait for.

### 3. Invite your manager

Go to Settings → Team & roles and press Invite member. This is not required, but do it now: your manager cannot help until they accept, and the dashboard shows whether they have.
