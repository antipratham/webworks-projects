Your outlet is live, but it cannot seat anyone until it has tables. These steps take about an hour, plus some waiting on Meta. Do them in this order.

### 4. Tables and areas

Go to Reservations → Floor plan → Edit layout.
- Add your areas first — main dining, terrace, private room.
- Add the tables in each area, with how many seats each one has.
- Mark tables that can be pushed together, so two tables for four can seat a party of six.
Do this first. Guests are offered only the seats your tables add up to. With no tables, nobody can book.

### 5. Shifts and booking rules

Go to Reservations → Settings. Add a shift for each service — at least lunch and dinner — with its days, hours and how long a table is held. Then check the booking rules:

| What it decides
| Starts at

| How far ahead a guest may book

| None

### 6. Menu

Go to Food Menu. Add dishes one by one, import a file or an export from your POS, or copy another outlet’s menu. Then print a QR code for each table from Food Menu → Table QR codes. See Food menu and QR ordering.

### 7. Your AI calling number and a test call

- Request your calling number from the outlet’s page in Settings → Outlets. We assign it; the dashboard shows “number requested” until then.
- In Settings → Calls, choose how calls are answered: staff first and then the AI host, the AI host alone, or a menu of options.
- Add at least one staff mobile to the ring order, so a call the AI passes on has someone to reach.
- Make a test call from AI Calls → Hunt group & routing → Try the AI host, and answer it.
Until a test call has been answered, the dashboard does not count your phone line as ready — and nor should you.

### 8. WhatsApp messages

Go to Settings → WhatsApp. Three messages go to guests automatically — the booking confirmation, the booking reminder and the missed-call message — and Meta must approve each one before it can be sent.
Submit them early. Approval takes minutes to a few hours, and a booking taken before then gets no WhatsApp confirmation.
Until you connect your own WhatsApp Business number (Connect with Meta), messages go out from the shared EatLabs number. That is fine for confirmations, but campaigns need your own number.

### 9. Email

Guests who give an email address also get confirmations, reminders and cancellations by email. These come from the EatLabs sender until you connect your own in Settings → Email — fine to start with.

### 10. Deposits — only if you take them

- Connect Razorpay in Settings → Payments. Guests pay you directly and the money settles to your bank — it never passes through us. Add your Razorpay keys first, then the webhook address the page shows you. Both are needed, or you will not hear when a guest pays. Start in test mode.
- Switch deposits on in Reservations → Settings → Deposits and write at least one rule: who pays, how much (per guest or a fixed amount), how long an unpaid booking is held, and your refund policy.
