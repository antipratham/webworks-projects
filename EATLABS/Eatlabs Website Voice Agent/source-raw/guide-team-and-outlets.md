## Your team and your outlets

Give each person exactly the access they need, and run every outlet from one account.

### Inviting people

- Go to Settings → Team & roles and press Invite member.
- Enter their email (and mobile, to send the invitation on WhatsApp too).
- Choose their role, and which outlets they work at.
- They open the invitation, set a password and sign in. The invitation lasts 7 days.

### Roles

Ready-made roles cover most restaurants: Owner, Outlet manager, Host, Marketing and Read-only. An outlet manager sees the dashboard for their outlets; a host starts on Reservations. You can adjust what each role can do in Settings → Team & roles → Roles.
Someone leaving? Remove them from the team and their access ends straight away. Settings → Audit log shows who changed what, and when.

### More than one outlet

Add each outlet in Settings → Outlets. Some things are shared across the group; others are set per outlet:
| Shared across all outlets
| Set for each outlet
| Guest profiles
| Phone number and AI host
| Loyalty points
| Floor plan and tables
| Segments and campaigns
| Menu
| Your team (with access per outlet)
| Opening hours and shifts
You can also run an event at several outlets at once, and promote a sister outlet to guests of another.
