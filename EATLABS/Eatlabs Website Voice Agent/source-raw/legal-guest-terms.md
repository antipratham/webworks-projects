### 1.What these terms cover

These terms apply when you use the guest side of EatLabs: the booking page, your booking link, the QR menu and table ordering, the waitlist, event pages and tickets, the loyalty account, the WhatsApp bot, and the AI host that may answer the restaurant’s phone.
EatLabs is operated by CHEVALBLEU LABS (OPC) PRIVATE LIMITED, Bengaluru, India. Using any of it means you accept these terms. If you do not, book by calling the restaurant and speaking to a person instead.

### 2.We are the software, not the restaurant

This is the most important thing on this page.
Your booking, your meal, your deposit and your bill are with the restaurant. EatLabs provides the system the restaurant runs on. We do not cook the food, set the prices, own the tables, take your money or decide the cancellation policy.
So:
- questions about a booking, a table, a bill, a refund, the food or the service go to the restaurant;
- the restaurant’s own terms, house rules, dress code and policies apply to your visit;
- we help with anything that is the software’s fault — a page that will not load, a confirmation that never arrived.

### 3.Bookings

- A booking is confirmed when the restaurant’s system confirms it — on the page, or by the WhatsApp or SMS message you get. A request that is still being held is not a confirmed table.
- Where a deposit is required, the table is held only until the payment window expires. Pay within it or the hold is released.
- Give accurate details. A wrong phone number means the confirmation, the reminder and the “we are running late” message all go to someone else.
- Tell us about allergies, and tell your server too. A dietary note on a booking reaches the kitchen, but the meal is prepared in a real kitchen by people. Speak to your server on arrival about anything that could make you ill. Do not rely on a note in an app for a serious allergy.
- Arrive on time. Restaurants hold a table for a limited period, which is theirs to set.
- Cancelling: use the link in your confirmation, or call. The cancellation window, whether a deposit is returned, and any no-show charge are the restaurant’s policy, shown to you before you commit.

### 4.Deposits, ticket payments and refunds

- You pay on Razorpay’s secure page, to the restaurant’s own merchant account. We never see your card, CVV or UPI PIN.
- The amount is always calculated by the restaurant’s system from its own rules. Nothing you can edit in a page changes what you are charged.
- Refunds follow the restaurant’s published policy and are issued by the restaurant. Processing time after that is Razorpay’s and your bank’s — usually 5 to 7 working days.
- If you were charged and your booking or ticket did not appear, contact the restaurant first; if it is a system fault, write to support@eatlabs.io with the payment reference and we will trace it.

### 5.The QR menu, table ordering and the AI concierge

- Menu items, prices, photographs, ingredient lists and dietary tags come from the restaurant. Availability changes during a service and the kitchen has the final word.
- The menu concierge is an AI assistant. It answers from the restaurant’s own dietary data and is guidance, not a medical or clinical assurance. For an allergy, ask a member of staff.
- An order placed at the table goes to the kitchen in your name and on your table’s bill. Order only for your own table.
- A shared table session lets several people at one table order onto one bill. Anyone who scans that table’s QR can add to it, so treat the session like the table itself — it ends when the bill is closed.

### 6.The AI host on the phone

- The restaurant’s phone may be answered by an AI. It will tell you what it is.
- Ask for a person at any time and the call is transferred, with a summary so you need not start again. If nobody is free, a callback is logged and the team is told to ring you.
- If the outlet records calls, you are told at the start of the call. Recordings are deleted after 90 days. See the Guest Privacy Notice.
- Never read out a card number, a CVV, an OTP or a UPI PIN on a call. No restaurant needs them, and nothing legitimate will ask you for them.
- Your phone carrier’s own call and data charges apply, as for any call.

### 7.Your booking link, and keeping it private

Your booking, order and ticket pages open from a signed link we send you. That link is the key — it opens that one booking and nothing else, and anyone holding it can see and cancel that booking.
Do not post it publicly. If you forward it, you have handed over the booking.

### 8.Event tickets

- A ticket is sold by the restaurant or the event host, not by us. Its terms, line-up, timings, age limits and refund policy are theirs.
- Each ticket carries a QR code, scanned once on entry. A screenshot works; a reused code does not.
- A cancelled or rescheduled event is the host’s to resolve. We will supply the records needed to sort it out.

### 9.Loyalty points

Where a restaurant runs a loyalty programme: points have no cash value, are not transferable, and cannot be exchanged for money. The earn rate, the tiers, the rewards and any expiry are the restaurant’s rules and it may change them, with notice to you. Points are per restaurant or group — they do not carry to another business, and they end if the restaurant closes its programme or stops using EatLabs.

### 10.How you must not use it

Do not:
- make bookings you do not intend to keep, in volume, or under someone else’s name or number;
- use automated tools to make bookings, hold tables or buy tickets;
- try to reach a booking, order, ticket or guest record that is not yours, including by guessing or altering a link;
- probe, scan or attack the service. Found a security problem? Tell us at security@eatlabs.io — we will thank you properly for a report made in good faith;
- abuse, threaten or harass restaurant staff through any channel the Platform carries. Messages are logged, and a restaurant may block you;
- upload anything unlawful, or a review or feedback you know to be false.
We may block access from an account, number or device that does any of this.

### 11.Availability

The guest app is provided as it is. We work to keep it up, but we do not guarantee it will always be available or free of faults. If it is down when you want to book, call the restaurant.

### 12.Our liability to you

Nothing in these terms limits a liability that cannot lawfully be limited, including for death or personal injury caused by negligence, or for fraud.
Beyond that: the restaurant is responsible for your dining experience — the food, the allergen handling, the service, the table, the price, the refund. Our responsibility is the software.
We are not liable for indirect or consequential loss. Where we are liable, our total liability to you is limited to the amount you paid through the Platform for the booking, order or ticket concerned, or ₹10,000, whichever is higher.

### 13.Changes

We may change these terms. The version on this page, with its effective date, is the one that applies. A material change will be reflected here before it takes effect, and continuing to use the guest app after that means you accept it.
