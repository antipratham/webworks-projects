### 11. Facebook, Instagram and Meta ads

Go to Settings → Meta config and press Connect with Meta. Campaigns, social posts, ads and Instagram messages all depend on this.
Connecting is not the last step. Choose which Facebook Page, which Instagram account and which ad account belong to which outlet. Without that, nothing gets published.

### 12. Google Business Profile

Also in Settings → Meta config. Needed to reply to Google reviews from EatLabs and to see your Google views, calls and searches. Connect it before switching on automatic replies in Reviews → Review settings.

### 13. Google Ads and Google Analytics — optional

Only if you already use them. Google Ads asks for your own account credentials; Meta ads work without it. For Google Analytics, connect and then choose the property for your website.

### The whole setup, in one list

#### Needed to open

- Outlet basics — address, guest phone, seats
- Opening hours — at least one day

#### Needed for bookings to work

- Tables and areas
- Shifts and booking rules
- A menu
- WhatsApp messages sent to Meta for approval

#### Then, in any order

- Manager invited, and accepted
- Calling number requested, ring order filled in, test call answered
- Your own WhatsApp number and email sender
- Razorpay, if you take deposits or sell event tickets
- Facebook Page, Instagram and ad account, for each outlet
- Google Business Profile, for review replies
- Google Ads and Analytics, if you use them
