## Events and ticketing

Sell tickets for a live music night, a tasting menu or a festival brunch, and check guests in at the door.

### Create an event

- Go to Events → Create event.
- Give it a date — once, or every week — and choose the whole venue, part of it, or shared seating.
- Add ticket types and prices, and extras like a drinks package or a front table.
- Share the event’s link or QR code on Instagram, WhatsApp or your website.
The tables an event uses are blocked automatically, so normal bookings never clash with it.

### Selling and check-in

- Guests pay by UPI or card and get their e-ticket on WhatsApp.
- At the door, scan each ticket in Events → Check-in. You can sell tickets at the door too.
- GST invoices and payout statements are made automatically. Add your GST details in Events → Billing.
Selling tickets needs Razorpay connected — see deposits.
