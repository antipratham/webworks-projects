## Reservations, floor plan and waitlist

Every booking from every channel in one agenda, with a live floor plan and a waitlist for walk-ins. A table can never be double-booked.

### Where bookings come from

Phone calls, the AI phone host, WhatsApp, Instagram, your website, Google, walk-ins, events, Zomato and Swiggy. They all check the same live availability, so two guests can never be given the same table. Each booking shows a small icon for where it came from.

### The agenda

Reservations → Agenda is the day’s list of bookings. From here you can:
- Add a booking by hand — for a guest who walks in or rings your landline.
- Open a booking to change the time, party size or table, add a note, or cancel it.
- Mark guests as arrived, seated or no-show.
- Export the day’s bookings.

### The floor plan

Reservations → Floor plan draws your tables as they are laid out, coloured by what is happening at each: free, seated, arriving soon or running late. If your POS is connected, you also see when a bill is open or paid. To change the layout, use Edit layout.

### The waitlist

When you are full, guests scan a QR code at the door to join the waitlist. They see their place in the queue on their phone, and get a “your table is ready” message on WhatsApp. Your team manages the queue in Reservations → Waitlist.

### Messages guests get automatically

- A confirmation when they book, and a message when a booking is changed or cancelled.
- Reminders 24 hours and 2 hours before, with Confirm and Cancel buttons.
- If WhatsApp cannot reach them, a text message instead.

### Deposits

Ask for a deposit by party size, weekday, shift or date. Guests pay by UPI or card, and refunds follow the policy you set. Setting it up is step 10 of Get ready for bookings.
