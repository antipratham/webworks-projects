## AI calls and the phone host

Your outlet gets its own number. Calls ring your staff first; when nobody answers, or you are closed, the AI phone host picks up and books the table.

### How a call is answered

- A guest calls your EatLabs number.
- Your staff’s phones ring — one by one or all together, up to six phones per outlet, with different people for different shifts.
- If nobody answers, or you are closed, the AI phone host answers in English, Hindi or Kannada. It checks live availability and books the table.
- If the guest needs a person, the AI passes the call to your staff with a short summary first. If still nobody answers, a callback is added to your list.

### Missed calls are not lost

A guest whose call was missed automatically gets a WhatsApp message with a booking link, with their number already filled in. See it working in AI Calls → Missed-call recovery.

### The AI Calls section

| Tab
| What you find there
| Call log
| Every call, live: who called, what they wanted, what happened, and the booking it made. Each has a written transcript.
| Callbacks due

| Missed callers, and whether the WhatsApp link brought them back.
| AI intents & quality
| What guests ask for most, and how well the AI handled it.
| Hunt group & routing
| Who rings, in what order, and the button to make a test call.
Card numbers and other sensitive numbers are removed from transcripts before they are saved.
