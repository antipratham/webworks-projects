Something not working as expected? Most problems are one missed setup step. Find yours below.

### Common problems

| What you see
| Why
| Fix it in
| A section says the outlet “isn’t live yet”
| Its address, phone, seats or opening hours are missing
| Settings → Outlets
| The floor plan is empty
| No tables have been added
| Reservations → Floor plan → Edit layout
| Guests are offered no times
| No tables, no shift on that day, or the booking rules rule it out
| Reservations → Settings
| A guest paid a deposit but the booking says unpaid
| The Razorpay webhook was not added
| Settings → Payments
| Marketing says nothing is connected
| Meta is connected but no Page is chosen for this outlet
| Settings → Meta config
| Guests are not getting confirmations
| The WhatsApp messages are not approved yet, or the guest gave no number or email
| Settings → WhatsApp
| The phone line is not marked ready
| No test call has been answered
| AI Calls → Hunt group & routing

### Getting help
