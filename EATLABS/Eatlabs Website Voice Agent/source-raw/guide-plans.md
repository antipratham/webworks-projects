### What Growth includes

- 24/7 AI voice receptionist · All languages
- AI-powered reservation management

- Real-time operational analytics and reports
- Role-based dashboards for your team

### How billing works

- Per venue, per month. Each outlet you run on EatLabs is one venue.
- No setup fee and no contract.
- GST is added to every invoice.
- WhatsApp messages are charged at Meta’s own rate, on top of the plan.
- Promotional codes. If we gave you one, enter it on the last step of the sign-up form.
- Your plan and invoices are in Settings → Billing.
Running a group or a chain? Talk to us about Enterprise.
