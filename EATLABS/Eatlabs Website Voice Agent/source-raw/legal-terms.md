If your Credits reach zero, metered features stop until you top up. Non-metered features, your data, and the ability to export it are unaffected. We will warn you before you get there, at the contact address on your account. Bookings already taken are never cancelled because of a Credit balance.

### 9.AI features, and their limits

The AI phone host, the WhatsApp bot, the menu concierge, AI review replies and AI Studio are built on large language models. You should understand four things about them.
- They can be wrong. Model output is generated, not retrieved from a rulebook. Review anything that goes out in your name, especially review replies and campaign copy, before or soon after it is published.
- The AI host’s powers are deliberately narrow. It can check availability and take, and change, the caller’s own booking. It cannot cancel or alter another guest’s booking, issue a refund, or reach any destructive function — those tools are not exposed to it at all, whatever a caller says to it.
- Allergens are not an AI matter. The menu concierge answers from the dietary data you supply. It is guidance for a diner, not a clinical assurance, and the guest web app tells diners to raise allergies with your staff. Keep your allergen data accurate and train your team accordingly.
- You may switch them off. Answering mode is per outlet. You can run hunt-group only, IVR, or AI answering, and you can turn recording, AI replies and the bot off independently.
We may change the underlying model providers and engines. Where that materially changes behaviour you depend on, we will tell you in advance.

- You must not use the Platform for autodialling, telemarketing campaigns or any calling pattern that breaches Indian telecom regulation. Outbound calling features are for your own guests and your own staff.

- Deliverability depends on the carrier and on Meta. We do not guarantee delivery, and Credits spent on an accepted send are not returned if the recipient’s device never shows it.

- Amounts are always computed by the server from your rules. A client never sends us a price.
- Deposits, cancellation terms and refunds are your policy, applied by your staff. A refund needs a reason and a permission, and is recorded in an append-only ledger.
- Your Razorpay keys and webhook secret are encrypted at rest and are never returned by the API.
- We are not a payment system operator and we do not hold, pool or remit your funds. Disputes, chargebacks and settlement timing are between you, your guest and Razorpay.
