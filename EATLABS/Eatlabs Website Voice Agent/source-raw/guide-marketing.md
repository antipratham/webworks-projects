- Marketing
Bring guests back with WhatsApp campaigns and automatic messages, publish to Instagram and Facebook, and see which ads turned into real bookings.

### WhatsApp campaigns

- Choose a message. Marketing messages must be approved by Meta first.
- Choose who gets it — a segment from the Guest CRM.
- Send it now or schedule it.
- Watch the report: sent, delivered, read, replied, and bookings made within 7 days.
Campaigns need your own WhatsApp number — see step 8 of Get ready for bookings.

### Automations

Marketing → Automations sends the right message at the right moment without anyone pressing a button — a birthday offer, a “we miss you” after a long gap, a thank-you after a first visit.

### Business Suite

Marketing → Business Suite puts your Instagram and Facebook ads next to the bookings and revenue they brought in, alongside your Google Business Profile views, calls, directions and search terms.

### Social posts

Marketing → Social Post lets you write, schedule and publish posts to Instagram and Facebook, see them on a calendar, and repeat a post every week.
All of this needs Meta connected first — see Start marketing.
