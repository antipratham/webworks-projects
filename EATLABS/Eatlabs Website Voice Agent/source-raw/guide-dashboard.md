## The dashboard

The first screen after you sign in: today’s business at a glance, and anything that needs you.

### Finding your way around

The menu down the left side of the dashboard has these sections. Each one has a page in this guide:
| Section
| What it is for
| Dashboard
| Today at a glance
| Reservations
| The agenda, floor plan and waitlist — see Reservations
| AI Calls
| Every call, answered or missed — see AI calls
| Guest CRM
| Everyone who has booked, called or visited — see Guest CRM
| Marketing
| Campaigns, social posts and ads — see Marketing
| Loyalty
| Points, rewards, offers and vouchers — see Loyalty
| Events
| Ticketed nights and check-in — see Events
| Reviews
| Google reviews and private feedback — see Reviews
| Food Menu
| Dishes and table QR codes — see Food menu
| Insights
| Reports — see Insights
| Settings
| Outlets, team, phone, WhatsApp, payments, billing and support
Each person sees only the sections their role allows. A host, for example, starts on Reservations and never sees billing.

### Overview

Dashboard → Overview shows today’s covers and bookings, how the floor looks right now, today’s calls and how many guests are new or returning. Each panel links to the full screen behind it.

### Needs attention

Dashboard → Needs attention lists what someone should act on — a guest running late, a callback that is due, a low review. Check it at the start of each service.
